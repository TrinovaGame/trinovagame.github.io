Trinova: Merge Puzzle — Support

A problem or an idea? The quickest way is the envelope button in the game (or Settings → Feedback): your message reaches me with your game version and device type, and my answer appears in the same window. You can also email me: dorukcagici2@gmail.com

Frequently asked

I bought "Remove ads" or the Supporter Pack and it does not show. Tap Settings → "Restore purchases". Being signed in with the same Apple account is enough.

Where is my progress kept? Only on your own device. Deleting the app deletes your progress too.

How do I unlock looks? By finishing Campaign worlds, reaching new levels in Survival, keeping a daily streak and with dust from chests.

I want my data deleted. Email me your player code (shown in the Feedback window); I will delete it within 30 days. Details: Privacy Policy.
